Booking calendar

Shaded = booked, hatched = open. Totals at the bottom are for booked spaces.
An activation space is booked in a month when the workbook lists a brand there. A media space is booked when the Media Tracker has an order on it that month. Every activation, store-owned and media space is listed, including ones with no booking all year. Brand fixtures are permanent, so they are left out. Lift is booked-space sales against the same month last year, so it needs both years' sales reports. Rent is counted only in booked months.
